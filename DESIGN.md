## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the poetic intimacy, bespoke elegance, and tactile warmth of high-end editorial wedding stationery. Conceived for the celebration of Maxi & Marina, the experience draws visual resonance from sheer draped textiles, organic botanical dried floral installations, limestone textures, and hand-pressed artisanal paper.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Romanticism**. It completely renounces corporate, SaaS, or clinical digital patterns in favor of generous negative space, gentle transitions, refined hairline borders resembling fine foil stamping, and an atmosphere of timeless, quiet luxury. The interaction model feels like unboxing a personalized physical invitation suite: calm, deliberate, reverent, and exquisitely styled.

## Colors

The color architecture is built directly from natural ivory, champagne silk, limestone, warm aged paper, and desaturated olive greenery:

- **Primary (`#2F2D2A` - Espresso Noir / Deep Charcoal Amber):** Replaces harsh digital pure black with a softened, deep vintage ink tone for typography and focal monograms.
- **Secondary (`#B89F74` - Soft Champagne Gold):** A muted, satin metallic leaf tone applied to hairline dividers, monograms, delicate badges, and decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#828774` - Desaturated Olive Mist):** An earthy, botanical sage tint evoking dried eucalyptus and wild olive foliage, reserved for status pills, subtle subheadings, and natural motifs.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Ivory Silk):** The foundational canvas color evoking heavy cotton rag paper. Paired with tonal surface variations:
  - Cream Linen (`#F4EFEA`): Interactive surfaces and softly tinted card containers.
  - Alabaster Veil (`#FFFFFF` with 75% opacity): Semi-sheer frosted layers mimicking organza and tulle.
  - Border Hairline (`#E8DFD5`): Soft tinted warm sand for non-invasive structure.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the dramatic editorial grace of high-contrast serif typography with the disciplined clarity of modern geometric sans-serif:

- **Playfair Display** commands the titles, couple's names, key dates, and emotional vignettes. It must frequently employ its italic posture (`script-flourish`) for romantic conjunctions (such as the ampersand `&`, "con la bendición de", or poetic excerpts).
- **Montserrat** provides pristine, highly legible body copy and micro-navigation. Rendered primarily in light weights (`300`) with generous letter-spacing, uppercase section headers (`label-caps`) echo high-fashion invitations and gallery signage.
- Standard paragraphs maintain loose line heights to invite slow, appreciative reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a single-column, centered narrative flow optimized primarily for mobile and tablet consumption, reflecting how wedding guests engage with RSVP links. On wide desktop screens, content is constrained to a maximum width of `680px` to maintain the intimate proportions of a printed invitation card.

- **Generous Vertical Breathing Room:** Sections are punctuated with `space-xl` gaps, avoiding cognitive clutter and letting each moment (Ceremony, Reception, Dress Code, RSVP) feel distinct.
- **Rhythm & Symmetry:** Elements utilize strict central alignment for formal ceremonial passages and balanced asymmetric layouts for secondary informational details like schedule timelines.
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Mobile layouts enforce a perimeter margin of `1.25rem`, scaling to `3rem` on desktop viewport wraps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy digital drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tactile paper layering, warm ambient halos, and sheer translucent overlays:

- **Chiffon Translucency:** Card panels and bottom floating action bars use `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur, evoking translucent layers of hanging bridal tulle over stone.
- **Warm Ambient Bloom:** Modals and focal cards rest over a tinted, diffused shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(184, 159, 116, 0.12), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(47, 45, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Deckle & Hairline Trims:** Containers are framed by delicate, 1px micro-borders in `#E8DFD5` or faded champagne `#D6C4A6`, reminiscent of gold-leaf foil pressing.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, architectural roundedness (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Default elements (cards, notification banners, RSVP form surfaces) use a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radius, preserving the crisp integrity of thick cut-edge cardstock.
- Circular elements are reserved strictly for monograms, countdown counters, and floating botanical motifs.
- Primary RSVP call-to-action buttons may use either an elegant soft border radius (`4px`) or an arched pill (`rounded-full`) exclusively when representing floating seals or wax-stamp buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (RSVP / Confirmar Asistencia):** Deep espresso background (`#2F2D2A`) with warm ivory typography (`#FBF9F5`), uppercase tracked letters, padding `14px 32px`, subtle gold hairline border on hover (`#B89F74`).
- **Secondary / Ghost (Agendar Calendario / Ver Mapa):** Transparent background, 1px champagne gold border (`#B89F74`), charcoal text, smooth hover transition to a soft cream fill (`#F4EFEA`).

### Cards & Itinerary Panels
- Structured as warm paper sheets with light sand backgrounds (`#F4EFEA`), delicate 1px border (`#E8DFD5`), and inner padding of `space-lg`.
- Timeline checkpoints utilize small olive or champagne circular dots connected by a 1px dashed or solid hairline.

### Inputs & RSVP Form Fields
- Minimalist underline or faint enclosed field styles. 
- Background: `#FAF7F2` with a 1px bottom border in `#D4CBC0`. On focus, the border transitions to `#B89F74` with zero garish outline rings. Placeholder copy is styled in light Montserrat (`#8F8B84`).

### Checkboxes & Attendance Radios
- Replaced with elegant custom selectors: subtle warm ivory pills that highlight with an olive mist (`#828774`) or gold leaf outline when selected.

### Custom Wedding Components
- **Monogram Seal:** Interlocking editorial initials "M & M" enclosed in a circular hairline emblem.
- **Countdown Timer:** Four distinct minimalist columnar counters (Días, Horas, Minutos, Segundos) set in Playfair Display with small uppercase sublabels.
- **Gift Registry / Dress Code Pill:** Tonal chips with olive/champagne iconography (e.g., formal attire icon, envelope/bank details) framed in hairline gold borders.
